<h1 align="center">Members' Council Order</h1>


### § 1 Purpose and Standing of the Members' Council

1. The members' council is a temporarily constituted organ of the association. Pursuant to § 7 para. 1 of the statutes, it is formed on an as-needed basis and represents the general assembly in the matters assigned to it.

2. It acts as an independent unit, is not bound by instructions in its decisions pursuant to § 7 para. 3 of the statutes, and is bound exclusively by the statutes and the association orders.

3. It supports the association's ability to act without permanently replacing the role of the general assembly.


### § 2 Responsibilities and Powers

1. Pursuant to § 7 para. 2 of the statutes, the members' council decides on:
    - matters submitted to it by the board due to urgency,
    - requests to initiate a vote of no confidence under § 8a para. 2 of the statutes,
    - remedies against resolutions and measures of the organs and mandated bodies.

2. It acts within the framework of the statutes and is bound by their fundamental principles.

3. It may not take decisions that are reserved exclusively to the general assembly by the statutes or by law.


### § 3 Occasion and Convening

1. The members' council is convened:
    - upon submission by the board, where a decision is required and a general assembly cannot be held in time,
    - upon request by a member or by the board to initiate a vote of no confidence,
    - upon the filing of a remedy on which, under the procedural order, the members' council decides.

2. Convening is carried out by a body mandated for this purpose; where no such body exists, by the board. Convening triggers the lottery procedure under § 4.

3. In proceedings under paragraph 1, second indent, as well as in proceedings concerning a board member, the board member concerned does not participate in the convening.

4. Convening may not be refused or delayed. The applicable periods are regulated by the procedural order.


### § 4 Composition and Lottery

1. Pursuant to § 7 para. 1 of the statutes, the members of the members' council are determined from among the members by a random, electronic procedure.

2. The procedure must be comprehensible, tamper-proof, and documented.

3. Pursuant to § 7 para. 3 of the statutes, persons involved in the respective matter and board members are excluded from the lottery.

4. Eligible to take part in the lottery are members who have declared their willingness to participate or who have actively taken part in the association's activities within a defined period. The criteria must be transparent, comprehensible, and accessible to all members.

5. The size of the members' council is determined according to the situation; it comprises at least 3 members and is an odd number.

6. Members may decline to participate for good cause. If, after the lottery, doubts arise as to the impartiality of a member, a declaration of partiality must be submitted in accordance with the procedural order.

7. In the cases of paragraph 6, a new lottery is held for the vacated seat.


### § 5 Term of Office

1. The members' council is formed exclusively for the respective decision at hand.

2. The mandate ends automatically upon conclusion of the proceedings within the meaning of the procedural order.


### § 6 Procedure

1. Deliberation may take place digitally, in hybrid form, or physically.

2. Decisions are made by simple majority unless the statutes provide otherwise.

3. Identity and expression of intent of the members follow § 6 para. 5 of the statutes.

4. Before a decision is taken on a matter concerning a person, a project, or a mandated body, that party must be given the opportunity to comment.

5. Decisions are issued as written resolutions and must be substantiated in a comprehensible manner.


### § 7 Confidentiality

1. Pursuant to § 5a para. 5 of the statutes, the members of the members' council are obliged to maintain confidentiality regarding personal circumstances that become known to them in the proceedings; this obligation continues beyond the end of membership or of the mandate.

2. Where data within the meaning of Articles 9 or 10 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 are processed in proceedings, the data subject must be informed of their right under § 10a para. 4 of the statutes.


### § 8 Transparency and Documentation

1. Decisions of the members' council must be documented in full in the case file.

2. The results must be made accessible to all members in a timely manner; where confidentiality obligations stand in the way, publication is made in anonymized form.

3. The basis for decisions should be presented in a comprehensible manner.


### § 9 Relationship to the General Assembly

1. Pursuant to § 7 para. 2 of the statutes, the general assembly may take over a decision of the members' council itself.

2. This does not apply to proceedings concerning the conduct of individual persons, nor to decisions on requests under § 8a para. 2 of the statutes. In such proceedings the members' council decides conclusively; the remedies under § 10a of the statutes remain unaffected.

3. The members' council is accountable to the general assembly insofar as paragraph 2 does not preclude this.


### § 10 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This members' council order, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
