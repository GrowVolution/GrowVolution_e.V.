<h1 align="center">Members’ Council Policy</h1>


### § 1 Purpose of the Members’ Council

1. The members’ council serves as a temporary representation of the general assembly in cases where a timely decision is required.

2. It supports the operational capability of the association without permanently replacing the role of the general assembly.


### § 2 Convening

1. The members’ council is convened as needed:
    - by the board,
    - or upon request of members.

2. The prerequisite is that:
    - a decision is required,
    - and a general assembly cannot be held in time.


### § 3 Composition and Selection

1. The members of the members’ council are selected from the membership through a random, electronic procedure.

2. The procedure must be transparent, tamper-resistant, and documented.

3. Members may decline participation for valid reasons.

4. The specific size of the members’ council is determined depending on the situation and must allow for effective decision-making.

5. Eligible for selection are members who have declared their willingness to participate or who have been active in the association within a defined period.

6. The criteria for eligibility must be transparent, comprehensible, and accessible to all members.


### § 4 Term of Office

1. The members’ council is formed exclusively for the respective decision case.

2. The mandate ends automatically upon completion of the decision.


### § 5 Tasks and Powers

1. The members’ council decides on matters assigned to it in accordance with § 7 of the statutes.

2. It acts within the framework of the statutes and is bound by their fundamental principles.

3. Its decisions are subject to subsequent review by the general assembly.


### § 6 Procedure

1. Deliberations may take place digitally, in hybrid form, or physically.

2. Decisions are made by simple majority, unless otherwise specified in the statutes.

3. The identity and expression of will of the members shall be verified in accordance with the provisions of the statutes.


### § 7 Transparency and Documentation

1. Decisions of the members’ council must be fully documented.

2. The results must be made accessible to all members in a timely manner.

3. The basis for decisions should be presented in a comprehensible manner.


### § 8 Relationship to the General Assembly

1. The general assembly may review, confirm, or overturn decisions of the members’ council.

2. The members’ council is accountable to the general assembly.


### § 9 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This members’ council policy, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
