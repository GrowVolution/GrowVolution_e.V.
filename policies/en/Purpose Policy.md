<h1 align="center">Purpose Policy</h1>


### § 1 Purpose of the Purpose Policy

1. This purpose policy governs the internal prioritization and concrete implementation of the tax-privileged purposes set out in § 2 para. 1 of the statutes.

2. It serves the gradual and realistic development of the association’s activities, taking into account the available resources.


### § 2 Principle of Implementation

1. The association follows a step-by-step approach in which structures, community, and projects are developed progressively.

2. All measures of the association must be designed in such a way that they can be assigned to one or more of the purposes defined in the statutes.


### § 3 Priority 1 – Building Community and an Educational Framework

1. In the initial phase, the focus lies on building an active and value-oriented community as the foundation for fulfilling the purposes of the statutes.

2. This is achieved in particular through:
    - regular meetings for exchange, reflection, and mutual support,
    - low-threshold events with an educational and discursive character,
    - open formats for integrating new interested individuals.

3. Events may include social elements, provided they clearly serve the promotion of education, exchange, or civic engagement.


### § 4 Priority 2 – Education and Democratic Discourse

1. The association places a particular focus on:
    - the promotion of education, public and vocational education,
    - the promotion of the democratic state system within the meaning of the statutes.

2. This includes in particular:
    - workshops, learning formats, and challenges,
    - discussion rounds, lectures, and exchange formats,
    - support for members in their personal and professional development.


### § 5 Priority 3 – Promotion of Civic Engagement

1. Building on a stable community, measures to promote civic engagement are developed.

2. This may include in particular:
    - joint projects with societal benefit,
    - support for non-profit initiatives,
    - encouragement of initiative within the community.


### § 6 Priority 4 – Science and Research

1. With increasing structural and financial development, the association promotes projects in the field of science and research.

2. This includes in particular:
    - cooperation with educational institutions,
    - conducting own research or pilot projects,
    - supporting science-related initiatives.


### § 7 Priority 5 – Development Cooperation

1. In the long term, the association expands its activities to include development cooperation.

2. This is carried out gradually, in particular through:
    - supporting existing projects,
    - establishing own initiatives,
    - knowledge transfer and capacity building.


### § 8 Resource Dependency

1. The scope and intensity of the individual measures depend on the available human, structural, and financial resources.

2. The association is not obliged to pursue all purposes simultaneously or to the same extent.


### § 9 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This purpose policy, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
