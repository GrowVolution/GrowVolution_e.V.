<h1 align="center">Privacy Protection Order</h1>


### § 1 Introduction

1. This order governs the handling of personal data by the association. Personal data refers to all data by which a natural person can be identified.

2. The controller responsible for data processing within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the association, represented by the board in accordance with § 26 BGB.


### § 2 Data Collection in Administrative Matters

1. When applying for membership, the following data is collected:
   - full name,
   - address,
   - email address,
   - telephone number (optional),
   - date and time of the application,
   - membership status.

   Processing is carried out in accordance with Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the purpose of processing the membership application and for establishing and managing the membership.

2. For the processing of donations, the following data is collected:
   - type and value of the donation,
   - date of the donation,
   - purpose of the donation,
   - if applicable, the payment method used.

   Personal data of the donating person is only collected if necessary or if a legal obligation exists.

   This applies in particular:
   - for donations exceeding €300 or upon request for the issuance of a donation receipt (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR),
   - for earmarked donations within the context of contractual relationships (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

   In all other cases, processing is carried out in compliance with the principle of data minimization.


### § 3 Data Collection in External Matters

1. The processing of personal data in the context of external services is governed by separate privacy policies.

2. Where more specific provisions exist, they take precedence over this order.

3. If processing is carried out by external service providers, this is done on the basis of a data processing agreement in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.

4. If the association processes personal data on behalf of third parties, it acts as a processor within the meaning of the GDPR.


### § 4 Processing in the Context of Purpose Fulfillment

1. Personal data collected in the context of association activities is processed for the purpose of carrying out those activities.

2. Processing is carried out:
   - on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if a contractual relationship exists,
   - or on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR if a legitimate interest of the association exists and no overriding interests of the data subject conflict with this.

3. Special categories of personal data are only processed if explicit consent in accordance with Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR has been obtained.

4. All personal data is processed confidentially and for specific purposes.


### § 5 Technical and Organizational Measures

1. The association implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data.

2. These include in particular:
   - access restrictions,
   - encryption of sensitive data,
   - regular review of security measures,
   - traceable logging of access.


### § 6 Rights of Data Subjects

1. Data subjects have the following rights:
   - right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
   - right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
   - right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
   - right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
   - right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
   - right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR),
   - right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

2. Where processing is based on legitimate interest, a right to object exists in accordance with Art. 21 GDPR.


### § 7 Data Retention

1. Personal data is only stored for as long as necessary for the respective purpose.

2. Statutory retention periods remain unaffected.

3. After the purpose ceases to apply or retention periods expire, the data is deleted.


### § 8 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This privacy protection order, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
