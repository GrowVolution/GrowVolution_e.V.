<h1 align="center">Board Policy</h1>


### § 1 Value of the Office

1. Each board member has equal rights and authorities internally.

2. In addition to their responsibility towards the general assembly, board members are obliged to ensure mutual transparency of their activities and to reflect them in accordance with the statutes.


### § 2 Distribution of Responsibilities

1. The responsibilities of the board members are distributed according to the composition of the board.

2. Delegation to association members or working groups is permitted through appropriate mandates.


### § 2a General Responsibilities of the Board

1. The board manages the day-to-day operations of the association.

2. The board decides on the admission of members.

3. The board represents the association in public and legal matters within the framework of the statutes.

4. The board implements resolutions of the members.

5. The board organizes events, resources, and funds and ensures their proper use.

6. The board maintains the association’s infrastructure and coordinates its further development.

7. The board ensures compliance with the statutes and their fundamental principles.

8. The board organizes general assemblies and ensures their proper conduct and documentation.


### § 2b 1st and 2nd Chairpersons

1. The chairpersons are responsible for signing and formally confirming actions with external legal effect.

2. They act within the framework of decisions made by the general assembly or the members’ council.

3. In case of minimal board composition, they jointly assume all responsibilities of the board and divide them among themselves.


### § 2c Treasurer

1. The treasurer is responsible for proper bookkeeping and documentation of financial transactions.

2. They coordinate financial transactions and the handling of financial processes.

3. They are responsible for issuing donation receipts and invoices.

4. The treasurer may be granted a power of attorney by the chairpersons for the performance of their duties. This is limited to financial matters and may be restricted or revoked by resolution of the general assembly or the board.


### § 2d Secretary

1. The secretary manages the association’s correspondence.

2. They are responsible for keeping minutes of meetings or ensuring that this is carried out.


### § 2e Permanent Representation

1. The permanent representative assumes the duties of a board member in the event of their absence, to the extent required.


### § 3 Board Meetings

1. The board may convene meetings for coordination and decision-making.

2. Outcomes relevant to members must be made transparent in a timely manner.


### § 4 Operational Powers

1. Board members acting externally are subject to a heightened obligation of transparency towards the general assembly.

2. Decisions of significant importance must be communicated openly prior to their implementation.

3. Members may raise objections to such decisions within a reasonable period.

4. If a substantial objection is raised, the matter must be referred to the general assembly or the members’ council for resolution.


### § 5 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This board policy, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
