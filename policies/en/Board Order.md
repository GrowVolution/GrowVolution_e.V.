<h1 align="center">Board Order</h1>


### § 1 Purpose of the Board Order

1. Pursuant to § 8 para. 8 of the statutes, this board order governs the working procedures and the allocation of responsibilities of the board, as well as the granting, design, and revocation of mandates.

2. It gives further shape to the statutes, in particular § 5a and § 8, without extending or restricting them.


### § 2 Value of the Office

1. Pursuant to § 8 para. 1 of the statutes, the board consists of the 1st chairperson, the 2nd chairperson, and the deputy.

2. Each board member has equal rights and authorities internally. There is no order of precedence between the positions.

3. In addition to their responsibility towards the general assembly, board members are obliged to ensure mutual transparency of their activities and to reflect them in accordance with the statutes.


### § 3 Distribution of Responsibilities

1. All three positions determine the distribution of responsibilities by mutual agreement and consensus. There is no fixed assignment of particular areas of responsibility to particular positions.

2. The distribution of responsibilities in force at any given time must be documented in the board file.

3. If no consensual distribution is reached, the board decides by simple majority.

4. Pursuant to § 8 para. 3 of the statutes, actions with external legal effect are always signed by two board members jointly. They are taken within the framework of decisions made by the general assembly or the members' council.


### § 4 General Responsibilities of the Board

1. The board manages the day-to-day operations of the association.

2. Pursuant to § 3 para. 4 of the statutes, the board decides on the admission of members.

3. The board represents the association in public and legal matters within the framework of the statutes.

4. The board implements resolutions of the members.

5. The board coordinates with the mandated bodies, coordinates their cooperation, and supervises their activity within the scope of the respective mandate.

6. The board organizes events, resources, and funds and ensures their proper use.

7. The board is responsible for proper bookkeeping, the documentation of financial transactions, and the issuance of invoices and donation receipts.

8. The board maintains the association's infrastructure and coordinates its further development.

9. The board ensures compliance with the statutes and their fundamental principles.

10. The board organizes general assemblies and ensures their proper conduct and documentation.


### § 5 Availability and Absence

1. Periods of leave, as well as periods in which a board member temporarily does not feel able to exercise their office, must be communicated to the other board members so that this can be taken into account in the work of the board.

2. The notification is informal and is made by a short message in the board chat or by email to vorstand@growvolution.org. It is mandatory within 3 days of the onset of that condition.

3. If the notification is omitted, the other board members may document this as a reprimand in the board file, irrespective of the personal circumstances involved.

4. From the third reprimand onwards, the board is obliged to request the members' council to initiate a vote of no confidence pursuant to § 8a para. 2 of the statutes.

5. The duties of a board member who is absent or relieved of duties pursuant to § 8a para. 3 of the statutes are assumed by the remaining board members to the extent required.


### § 6 Board Meetings

1. The board may convene meetings for coordination and decision-making.

2. Outcomes relevant to members must be made transparent in a timely manner.


### § 7 Board File

1. The board maintains a board file. It is permanently accessible to all members and also serves as a means of accountability towards the general assembly.

2. The board file documents in particular:
    - the distribution of responsibilities in force under § 3,
    - board resolutions,
    - mandates and powers of attorney granted, amended, and revoked under § 8,
    - decisions on requests under § 9,
    - reprimands under § 5.

3. Where entries concern personal circumstances, the confidentiality obligation under § 5a para. 5 of the statutes applies; in such cases the documentation is kept in anonymized form.


### § 8 Delegation and Mandates

1. Pursuant to § 5a para. 1 of the statutes, delegation does not constitute hierarchical superiority; it is a mandate with a reporting obligation to the general assembly.

2. The board is entitled to delegate day-to-day operations freely. For this purpose it grants mandates and powers of attorney to members, working groups, branches, projects, and other administrative units.

3. The delegation of operational activities is not a fundamental decision within the meaning of § 5a para. 3 of the statutes. Prior consideration by the general assembly or the members' council is not required; transparent documentation in the board file, which is accessible to all members, is sufficient.

4. Every mandate is bound to a specific purpose, limited in substance, and revocable by the granting body as well as at any time by the general assembly or the members' council.

5. Powers of attorney and mandates must not circumvent the principle of decentralization under § 5a of the statutes. Mandates are impermissible in particular where they
    - withdraw fundamental decisions or major financial or resource decisions from determination by the general assembly or the members' council,
    - establish hierarchical superiority or authority to issue instructions beyond the scope of the mandate,
    - suspend or restrict reporting, notification, and transparency obligations.

6. In carrying out operational activities of the board on its behalf, every mandated body is bound by the statutes and the association orders. No powers may be transferred to it that go beyond those held by the board itself.

7. The board remains accountable to the general assembly for the delegated responsibilities.

8. Onward delegation by a mandated body is permissible only where the mandate expressly provides for it. It must be reported to the board without delay; the board holds a right of veto.

9. Mandates granted, amended, and revoked must be documented in the board file without delay. The documentation covers at least the mandated body, the scope of the mandate, its commencement, and any time limit.

10. Mandates for the performance of board responsibilities end at the latest upon expiry of the board's term of office unless granted anew. Mandates to working groups, branches, and projects continue to exist independently of the term of office.


### § 9 Requests to Establish Mandated Bodies

1. Members may request the board to establish a working group, a branch, a project, or another mandated body.

2. The board is obliged to accept such requests, to decide on them, and to approve them as a matter of principle unless serious objections stand in the way. Approval means the granting of a mandate that confers on the requested body the operational powers required to fulfil its task.

3. The decision is issued as a written resolution. Where a request is not granted in full, the decision must be substantiated and the remedy under § 10a of the statutes is available.

4. The criteria governing the obligation to grant a mandate, the period within which a decision must be taken, and the handling of objections, formal defects, and corrections are regulated by the procedural order.

5. The substantive requirements for requests to establish projects, working groups, and branches are regulated by the standing orders.


### § 10 Operational Powers and Transparency

1. Pursuant to § 8 para. 5 of the statutes, the board is obliged to disclose all of its operational and independently made decisions to the general assembly without being asked.

2. Decisions of significant importance must be communicated openly prior to their implementation.

3. Members may raise objections to such decisions within a reasonable period.

4. If a substantial objection is raised, the matter must be referred to the general assembly or the members' council for resolution.

5. When taking decisions within the scope of their mandate, mandated bodies are subject to the same notification and transparency obligations as the board.


### § 11 Entry into Force and Amendments

1. This board order, as well as any amendments, enters into force upon resolution by the general assembly.

2. It may be amended within the framework of the statutes to respond to internal and external developments.
