## 🔱 Branching rules and meaning

To keep the development of our internal architecture clean, we provide a short introduction to our branching system:

1. main – Contains the adopted versions of our governance documents. Can only be changed via pull requests. A pull request initiates the internal adoption process.

2. unadopted – The actively maintained branch that contains the current state of our documents before adoption.

3. policies/* – Contains newly created policies and their development before they are merged into the unadopted branch.

4. statutes – The branch where our statutes are developed. Statutes are not developed in the unadopted branch because they must be approved by a notary and adopted by the court before they can be merged into the main branch.

5. cps – Development of our Certification Practice Statements. Can be merged without approval; only members of the board or the administrative unit "Digital Infrastructure" (DI) are allowed to merge from here.
